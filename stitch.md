## 🎨 Design System: DeployHub Engineering System

### 🌌 Visual Philosophy

Built for high-performance DevOps professionals. Prioritizes a **"precise engineering"** aesthetic:

- **Minimalism & Glassmorphism:** Layered surfaces, subtle depth.
- **Atmosphere:** cinematic, high information density, absolute control.
- **Elevation:** Tonal layers and backdrop blurs (`12px`) instead of drop shadows.
